**Duration / Duración:** 60 minutes

> **💡 Tutor Tip for Lewis (Consejo para el tutor):**  
> In Lesson 2, Bernie practiced formal radio SitReps ("The road is impassable", "Requesting medevac"). In Lesson 3, the context shifts directly to the **ground zero field perimeter**. Here, English is spoken loudly, with urgency and absolute clarity.  
> Teach Bernie that in crowd safety and search-and-rescue, polite English ("Could you please consider stepping back?") is dangerous. International responders use **loud, direct imperative commands** ("Step back!", "Clear the building!"). For trapped survivors, she needs a **calm, steady, reassuring voice** ("Help is on the way. Stay calm, do not move"). Connect these directly to her daily field experience in Argentina.

### 1. ⚠️ Crowd Safety & Perimeter Control (To bystanders and survivors)
*Comandos imperativos, firmes y cortos para asegurar el perímetro frente a derrumbes secundarios, fugas de gas o cables caídos:*

| English Command | Español | Pronunciación aprox. | Operational Context |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **"Step back! Keep clear of the building!"** | ¡Atrás! Aléjense del edificio / Mantengan despejado el edificio | *STEP BAK! KIIP KLÍAR ov the BÍL-ding!* | Para empujar a curiosos y familiares fuera del radio de caída de muros. |
| **"Do not enter! The structure is unstable / dangerous."** | ¡No entren! La estructura está inestable / peligrosa | *DU NOT ÉN-ter! The STRÁK-cher iz an-STÉI-bl / DÉIN-dje-ras* | Evita que voluntarios sin equipo ingresen a zonas con peligro de colapso. |
| **"Move to the safe zone immediately!"** | ¡Vayan a la zona segura de inmediato! | *MUUV tu the SÉIF zoun i-MÍ-diat-li!* | Orden directa para evacuar hacia plazas abiertas, parques o puntos seguros. |
| **"Watch out for falling debris!"** | ¡Cuidado con los escombros que caen! | *WOTCH ÁUT for FÓ-ling de-BRÍI!* | *Atención:* En **debris** la "s" final es muda (*de-bríi* = escombros / cascotes). |
| **"Turn off the gas / power!"** | ¡Corten el gas / la electricidad! | *TERN OFF the GAS / PÁU-er!* | Prevenir explosiones secundarias o electrocución por cables sueltos. |
| **"Clear the building!"** | ¡Desalojen el edificio! / ¡Todos afuera! | *KLÍAR the BÍL-ding!* | Comando operativo estándar. Mucho más directo y urgente que *"leave the building"*. |

### 2. 🔍 Rapid Assessment & Locating Victims (Direct inquiries & reassurance)
*Preguntas directas para determinar quién está atrapado y frases tranquilizadoras para contener a las víctimas:*

| English Inquiry / Reassurance | Español | Pronunciación aprox. | Operational Context |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **"Is anyone trapped inside?"** | ¿Hay alguien atrapado adentro? | *iz ÉN-i-wan TRAPT in-SÁID?* | La primera pregunta al llegar al edificio, dirigida a testigos o sobrevivientes. |
| **"How many people were in the building?"** | ¿Cuántas personas había en el edificio? | *HAU MÉ-ni PÍI-pl wer in the BÍL-ding?* | Clave para saber cuántas víctimas buscar (ej.: "There were 5 people"). |
| **"Can anyone hear me? Make noise if you can!"** | ¿Alguien me escucha? ¡Hagan ruido si pueden! | *kan ÉN-i-wan HÍAR mi? MEIK NOIS if yu KAN!* | Grito hacia los escombros para obtener respuesta verbal o golpes (tapping). |
| **"Help is on the way. Stay calm, do not move."** | La ayuda está en camino. Mantengan la calma, no se muevan. | *HELP iz on the WEI. STEI KAAM, DU NOT MUUV* | Frase vital para evitar que el herido se desespere y provoque un nuevo derrumbe. |
| **"Who is injured? Can you walk?"** | ¿Quién está herido? ¿Puede caminar? | *HUU iz ÍN-djerd? kan yu WOK?* | Evaluación rápida de triaje: separa heridos ambulatorios (verdes) de graves. |
| **"Describe where you are!"** | ¡Describí dónde estás! (¿Qué ves?) | *dis-KRÁIB hwer yu AR!* | Pedir a la víctima que indique si ve luz, huecos, tuberías o muebles. |
| **"Everyone quiet! Listen!"** | ¡Silencio todos! ¡Escuchen! | *ÉV-ri-wan KWÁI-et! LÍ-sn!* | Orden a los socorristas y multitud para detectar golpes o silbatos. |
| **"Stay calm and breathe."** | Mantené la calma y respirá. | *STEI KAAM and BRIITH* | Evita ataques de pánico (*nervous breakdown*) mientras llega el equipo de corte. |

## Part 3: Field Commander Rules & Syntax Formulas (10 mins)

### Rule 1: The Imperative for Emergency & Crowd Control (Verbo + Destino)
En emergencias, las normas de cortesía habituales en inglés se eliminan por razones de seguridad de vida o muerte:
* ❌ *Don't say:* "Could you please step back if it's not a problem?" *(Demasiado suave, la gente no reacciona).*
* ✅ **Say:** **"Step back! Keep clear of the building!"** *(Voz de mando clara y directa).*
* ❌ *Don't say:* "I want you to leave the room."
* ✅ **Say:** **"Clear the building! Everyone out now!"**

### Rule 2: Reassurance Protocol for Trapped Victims (Protocolo de Contención)
Cuando una víctima atrapada responde, utilizá la tríada de contención:
```
[CONFIRM HELP] ➔ [PREVENT MOVEMENT] ➔ [REGULATE BREATHING]
"Help is on the way." ➔ "Do not move." ➔ "Stay calm and breathe."
```
*Hablarle constantemente a la víctima evita el colapso emocional (*nervous breakdown*) y mantiene sus signos vitales estables hasta que el equipo USAR (Búsqueda y Rescate Urbano) penetre la losa.*

### Rule 3: The Pronunciation of "Debris" & "Whistle"
* **Debris** = `/dəˈbriː/` o `/de-bríi/`. ¡La letra **S** al final **nunca se pronuncia**!
* **Whistle** = `/ˈwɪs.əl/` o `/wí-sl/`. La letra **T** es muda (*silbato de emergencia* del kit).

## Part 5: Roleplay Scenarios / Simulaciones Operativas (15 mins)

### 📢 Scenario 1: Perimeter Control & Secondary Collapse Danger
* **Learner's Role:** Bernie — Ground Operations Lead securing a collapsed 3-story bakery after an earthquake aftershock.
* **Tutor's Role:** Lewis — Panicked family member trying to push past the yellow tape to find his brother.
* **Situation Card:**
  * The building walls are visibly leaning (*unstable structure*).
  * Mortar and bricks are falling (*falling debris*).
  * A gas leak is suspected near the main valve.
* **Bernie's Mission:**
  1. Halt Lewis immediately: *"Step back! Keep clear of the building!"*
  2. Explain the danger firmly: *"Do not enter! The structure is unstable. Watch out for falling debris!"*
  3. Direct him and bystanders: *"Move to the safe zone immediately!"*
  4. Order safety utility cut: *"Turn off the gas and power!"*
* **Tutor Opener:**  
  *(Panicked)* *"My brother is inside! Let me through, I need to get him out right now!"*

---

### 🔍 Scenario 2: Acoustic Search & Locating Victims Under Rubble
* **Learner's Role:** Bernie — Search & Rescue Lead conducting an acoustic search cycle.
* **Tutor's Role:** Lewis — Trapped survivor buried beneath broken drywall and furniture.
* **Situation Card:**
  * Rescue team has cut engine and generator noise.
  * You need total silence around the pile.
  * You make voice contact and provide mental grounding.
* **Bernie's Mission:**
  1. Demand complete silence: *"Everyone quiet! We need to listen!"*
  2. Call out: *"Can anyone hear me? Make noise if you can!"*
  3. Reassure the victim upon hearing a knock: *"Help is on the way. Stay calm, do not move."*
  4. Assess injuries and position: *"Who is injured? Can you walk? Describe where you are."*
  5. Keep them calm: *"Stay calm and breathe. We are cutting through now."*
* **Tutor Opener:**  
  *(Tapping sound: tap... tap... tap... followed by a weak shout)*  
  *"Hello?! I'm in here! Please get me out, I can't breathe well!"*

### 📋 Scenario 3: Rapid Information Gathering from Building Manager
* **Learner's Role:** Bernie — Incident Coordinator arriving at a collapsed community center.
* **Tutor's Role:** Lewis — Shocked building manager standing on the sidewalk.
* **Bernie's Mission:**
  1. Direct inquiry: *"How many people were in the building?"*
  2. Clarify trapped victims: *"Is anyone trapped inside? Where were they located?"*
  3. Inquire on injuries: *"Who is injured? Can they walk?"*
  4. Secure utilities: *"Where is the main switch? Turn off the gas and power immediately!"*
* **Tutor Opener:**  
  *"I'm the building manager! The ceiling just came down on the main meeting room!"*
