# 🚨 English Lesson 1: Emergency & Disaster Relief
# Lección de Inglés 1: Ayuda y Socorro en Desastres

**Level / Nivel:** Beginner – Pre-Intermediate (Principiante / Pre-intermedio)  
**Duration / Duración:** 60 minutes  
**Target Learner / Estudiante:** Professional disaster relief / humanitarian aid worker learning English for international field coordination.

---

> **💡 Tutor Tip (Consejo para el tutor):**  
> Since she already does this job in her native language, don't teach heavy grammar. In crisis situations, native speakers and international relief teams use short, clear, and direct sentences. Connect the English terms directly to the operational procedures she already knows.

---

## Part 1: Warm-Up / Calentamiento (5–10 mins)

Ask these questions in Spanish/English to get her talking and thinking about her real work:

1. **What disasters happen most in your country?**  
   *(¿Qué desastres ocurren más en tu país?)*  
   * *Keywords:* flood (inundación), earthquake (terremoto), hurricane / storm (huracán / tormenta), landslide (deslizamiento de tierra), fire (incendio), drought (sequía).
2. **In your job, what do you do?**  
   *(En tu trabajo, ¿qué haces?)*  
   * *Examples:* Do you distribute food and water? Do you provide first aid? Do you coordinate teams?
3. **Why do you need English in your job?**  
   *(¿Por qué necesitas inglés en tu trabajo?)*  
   * *Examples:* Talking to foreign NGOs, reading disaster situation reports, assisting foreign tourists or international coordinators.

---

## Part 3: Plug-and-Play Sentence Templates / Plantillas Rápidas (10 mins)

Explain that in an emergency, she only needs these 4 essential patterns:

### Pattern 1: Reporting Numbers & Status (Reportar cifras y estado)
* *"There are **[number]** people at **[place]**."*  
  *(Hay [número] personas en [lugar].)*
* *"**[Number]** people are **[injured / missing / trapped]**."*  
  *([Número] personas están [heridas / desaparecidas / atrapadas].)*
* **Example:** *"There are 150 people at the school. Ten people are injured."*

### Pattern 2: Requesting Supplies (Pedir suministros)
* *"We urgently need **[item]** at **[place]**."*  
  *(Necesitamos urgentemente [artículo] en [lugar].)*
* *"We have a shortage of **[water / blankets / medicine]**."*  
  *(Tenemos escasez de [agua / mantas / medicinas].)*

### Pattern 3: Reporting Problems & Road Conditions (Reportar problemas y vías)
* *"The **[road / bridge]** is **[blocked / flooded / broken]**."*  
  *(La carretera / el puente está [bloqueado / inundado / destruido].)*
* *"The power is **down**."*  
  *(No hay electricidad / Se cayó la energía.)*

### Pattern 4: Giving Direct Instructions (Instrucciones directas al público)
* *"Please **move** to high ground."* *(Por favor, suban a zonas altas.)*
* *"**Stay** away from the building."* *(Aléjense del edificio.)*
* *"**Wait** here in line for supplies."* *(Esperen aquí en fila para los suministros.)*

## Part 4: Practice Exercises / Ejercicios Prácticos (15 mins)

### Exercise A: Which word do you need? (¿Qué palabra necesitas?)
Choose from:
> **`evacuate`** | **`shortage`** | **`trapped`** | **`assess`** | **`distribute`** | **`shelter`**

1. A tree fell on a house. A man is inside and cannot open the door. He is __________.  
   *(Un árbol cayó sobre una casa. Un hombre está adentro y no puede abrir la puerta. Él está...)*
2. You have 200 people, but only 20 bottles of water. You have a water __________.  
   *(Tienes 200 personas pero solo 20 botellas de agua. Tienes una... de agua.)*
3. The river is rising fast. Everyone must leave their homes now! We must __________.  
   *(El río está subiendo rápido. ¡Todos deben salir de sus casas ya! Debemos...)*
4. You arrive at a village after an earthquake. First, you look around to check how many houses fell and who is hurt. You __________ the situation.  
   *(Llegas a un pueblo tras un terremoto. Primero miras alrededor para ver casas caídas y heridos. Tú... la situación.)*
5. People have no houses tonight, so they sleep inside the sports center. The sports center is a __________.  
   *(La gente no tiene casa esta noche, así que duermen en el polideportivo. El polideportivo es un...)*
6. Your team opens the boxes of rice and gives one bag to each family. You __________ the food.  
   *(Tu equipo abre las cajas de arroz y le da una bolsa a cada familia. Ustedes... la comida.)*

---

### Exercise B: Upgrade the "Broken English" (Mejorar el inglés básico)
Turn these rough phrases into clear professional sentences using the templates:

1. *Rough:* "Water no have! Many people thirsty in Tent 3!"  
   * **Target:** *"We have a **shortage** of **water** in Tent 3."*  
   *(Tenemos escasez de agua en la Carpa 3.)*

2. *Rough:* "Road tree fall down. Truck cannot go."  
   * **Target:** *"The road is **blocked**. The truck cannot pass."*  
   *(La carretera está bloqueada. El camión no puede pasar.)*

3. *Rough:* "Five person hurt very bad! Need doctor fast!"  
   * **Target:** *"Five people are **injured**. This is **urgent**! We need a doctor."*  
   *(Cinco personas están heridas. ¡Esto es urgente! Necesitamos un médico.)*

4. *Rough:* "You not stay here! Go school! School safe!"  
   * **Target:** *"Please **evacuate** this area. Go to the **shelter** at the school."*  
   *(Por favor evacuen esta área. Vayan al albergue en la escuela.)*

## Part 5: Roleplay Scenarios / Simulaciones (15 mins)

### 📻 Scenario 1: Radio Call to Headquarters (Llamada de radio a la central)
* **Learner's Role:** Field Worker at **Sunny Hill School Shelter** after a major flood.
* **Tutor's Role:** International NGO Coordinator on the radio.
* **Situation Card (Ficha de situación):**
  * **Location:** Sunny Hill School
  * **People:** 300 displaced people *(300 personas desplazadas)*
  * **Injuries:** 12 injured people needing first aid and a doctor *(12 heridos)*
  * **Supplies:** Severe shortage of clean water and baby food *(Escasez de agua y comida para bebés)*
  * **Access:** Main bridge is flooded; use North Road *(Puente principal inundado; usar carretera norte)*
* **Tutor Opener:**  
  *"Hello! This is Sarah from the International Relief Team. What is the situation at Sunny Hill School? What do you need?"*

---

### 📢 Scenario 2: Assisting a Confused Foreigner (Ayudando a un turista)
* **Learner's Role:** Relief Officer in the field.
* **Tutor's Role:** Scared English-speaking tourist after an earthquake trying to enter a damaged hotel.
* **Learner's Goals:**
  1. Tell the person not to enter the hotel: *"Do not enter. It is dangerous."*
  2. Direct them to the safe zone: *"Please evacuate to the safe zone at the city park."*
  3. Explain supplies: *"We will distribute water and blankets there."*
