## Part 3: Plug-and-Play SitRep Sentence Templates / Plantillas de Reporte de Situación (10 mins)

Teach Bernie that international coordinators communicate in a 4-part **SITREP (Situation Report)** format:

```
[LOCATION] ➔ [ACCESS / CONDITIONS] ➔ [CASUALTIES / TRIAGE] ➔ [URGENT LOGISTICS NEED]
```

### Pattern 1: Reporting Access & Roads (Reportar rutas y accesos)
* *"The road to **[Place]** is **[blocked / collapsed / impassable]**."*  
  *(La ruta hacia [Lugar] está [bloqueada / colapsada / intransitable].)*
* *"The route is **passable for 4x4 only**."*  
  *(La ruta es transitable solo para 4x4.)*
* *"Our ETA is **[15 minutes / 1 hour]**."*  
  *(Nuestra hora estimada de llegada es en [15 minutos / 1 hora].)*

### Pattern 2: Medical Triage Updates (Reporte de heridos por nivel)
* *"We have **[number] critical patients** with **[head injuries / fractures / severe bleeding]**."*  
  *(Tenemos [número] pacientes críticos con [traumatismo craneal / fracturas / hemorragias graves].)*
* *"Requesting immediate **medevac** for **[number]** people."*  
  *(Solicitamos evacuación médica inmediata para [número] personas.)*

### Pattern 3: Distribution & Capacity (Puntos de entrega y capacidad)
* *"The distribution point is active at **[Place]**."*  
  *(El punto de distribución está activo en [Lugar].)*
* *"Shelter capacity is **[number]** people. It is **[full / at 50%]**."*  
  *(La capacidad del albergue es de [número] personas. Está [lleno / al 50%].)*

### Pattern 4: Dispatch Orders (Órdenes de despacho)
* *"Please **dispatch [ambulances / 4x4 trucks / water tankers]** to **[Sector / Location]**."*  
  *(Por favor despachen [ambulancias / camionetas 4x4 / camiones cisterna] a [Sector / Lugar].)*

## Part 4: Practice Exercises / Ejercicios Prácticos (15 mins)

### Exercise A: Field Triage & Logistics Match (¿Qué término usás?)
Choose from:
> **`medevac`** | **`impassable`** | **`power outage`** | **`critical`** | **`ETA`** | **`dispatch`**

1. A mudslide covered the highway. Large supply trucks cannot drive through. The highway is __________.  
   *(Un alud de barro cubrió la autopista. Los camiones no pueden pasar. La autopista está...)*
2. A patient was crushed by a concrete beam, has severe chest bleeding, and is unconscious. This patient is in __________ condition.  
   *(Un paciente fue aplastado por una viga, sangra del pecho y está inconsciente. Está en estado...)*
3. The storm cut all electricity cables to the hospital. There is a complete __________.  
   *(La tormenta cortó los cables del hospital. Hay un... total.)*
4. A patient is dying and the local field clinic cannot do surgery. You call an emergency helicopter for a __________.  
   *(Un paciente está en riesgo de vida y la clínica de campo no puede operar. Pedís un helicóptero para una...)*
5. The convoy driver radios: *"We are on Highway 9. Our __________ is 14:30 hours."*  
   *(El conductor del convoy avisa por radio: "Estamos en Ruta 9. Nuestra... es a las 14:30 horas".)*
6. Operations base says: *"Copy that. We will __________ two water tankers immediately."*  
   *(La base dice: "Copiado. Vamos a... dos camiones cisterna de inmediato".)*

### Exercise B: Upgrade the "Broken English" to Field SitRep
Improve these rough messages into professional disaster coordination English:

1. *Rough:* "Road broken! Truck with doctor cannot drive!"  
   * **Target:** *"The road is **collapsed**. It is **impassable** for supply trucks."*  
   *(La ruta está colapsada. Está intransitable para camiones de suministros.)*

2. *Rough:* "One man head blood very bad, he sleep, no wake up! Need helicopter fast!"  
   * **Target:** *"One patient is **critical and unconscious** with severe **head bleeding**. We request an immediate **medevac**."*  
   *(Un paciente está crítico e inconsciente con hemorragia grave en la cabeza. Solicitamos medevac inmediato.)*

3. *Rough:* "School have place only for 100 people, now have 200 people inside!"  
   * **Target:** *"The school shelter is **over capacity**. Maximum capacity is 100 people."*  
   *(El albergue de la escuela superó su capacidad. La capacidad máxima es de 100 personas.)*

4. *Rough:* "What time the food truck come here?"  
   * **Target:** *"What is the **ETA** of the food truck at the distribution point?"*  
   *(¿Cuál es la hora estimada de llegada del camión de comida al punto de distribución?)*

## Part 5: Roleplay Scenarios / Simulaciones Operativas (15 mins)

### 📻 Scenario 1: Radio SitRep to UN / International Operations Center (OOC)
* **Learner's Role:** Bernie — Field Operations Lead at **Sector 7 (River Valley)** after a Category 4 Hurricane.
* **Tutor's Role:** Lewis — UN Disaster Assessment and Coordination (UNDAC) Liaison on the radio.
* **Situation Card (Ficha Operativa de Bernie):**
  * **Location:** Sector 7 Community Center
  * **Road Access:** Main bridge collapsed; route is passable for **4x4 vehicles only**.
  * **Power / Utilities:** Total power outage; running on backup generator; tap water is contaminated.
  * **Casualties / Triage:** 3 **critical** patients (1 fracture, 2 severe bleeding needing medevac), 15 **minor** walking wounded.
  * **Logistics Needs:** Request dispatch of 1 medevac helicopter and clean drinking water.
* **Tutor Opener:**  
  *"Sector 7 Field Lead, this is UN Operations Base. We need your SitRep. What is your road access, power status, and casualty priority?"*
