## Part 2: Core Operational Vocabulary & Phrasing (15–20 mins)

Practice pronunciation with Bernie using the phonetic transcriptions adapted to Argentine Spanish speakers.

### 1. 📐 INSARAG / USAR Structural Search Markings (El Marcado de Edificios 2×2m)
*Cuando equipos internacionales de rescate (USAR) evalúan un edificio colapsado, pintan una cruz de 2x2 metros con spray naranja o amarillo en la fachada. Cada cuadrante tiene un significado estricto:*

```
                 [ TOP / ARRIBA ]
            Date & Time Entered / Exited
            "26 OCT - 14:00 IN / 16:30 OUT"
                       |
 [ LEFT / IZQUIERDA ]  |  [ RIGHT / DERECHA ]
    Rescue Team ID     |     Hazards Identified
      "ARG-01"         |   "GAS LEAK / LIVE POWER /
                       |    SECONDARY COLLAPSE RISK"
                       |
                [ BOTTOM / ABAJO ]
            Victims Extricated & Status
             "L-2 / D-0" (2 Live, 0 Deceased)
```

| Operational Phrase | Español | Pronunciación aprox. | Contexto de Uso |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **"The structure is marked as searched."** | La estructura está marcada como revisada. | *the STRÁK-cher iz MARKT az SERCHT* | Confirma que un equipo ya inspeccionó el lugar. |
| **"Hazards identified: confirmed gas leak."** | Peligros identificados: fuga de gas confirmada. | *HÁ-zards ai-DÉN-ti-faid: kon-FERMD GAS LIIK* | Letrero en el cuadrante derecho para alertar a otros grupos. |
| **"Live wires / live power lines."** | Cables con corriente / cables con electricidad viva. | *LAIV WÁI-ers / LAIV PÁU-er lains* | Riesgo crítico de electrocución cerca de agua o fierros. |
| **"Risk of secondary collapse."** | Riesgo de colapso secundario (réplica). | *RISK ov SÉ-kon-de-ri ko-LÁPS* | Muros agrietados que pueden caer por vibración o réplicas. |
| **"Two live victims extricated."** | Dos víctimas con vida extraídas/rescatadas. | *TUU LAIV VÍK-tims ÉKS-tri-kei-tid* | En el cuadrante inferior (*L = Live / Vivos*). |
| **"Zero deceased."** | Cero fallecidos. | *ZÍI-rou di-SÍIST* | En el cuadrante inferior (*D = Deceased / Fallecidos*). |

## Part 3: Field Rules & Communication Syntax (10 mins)

### Rule 1: The ETA Formula (Preguntar y Responder sobre Tiempos de Llegada)
En situaciones de rescate, el tiempo es crítico (*golden hour*). Bernie debe dominar la estructura para preguntar y responder:
* **Asking:** `"What is the ETA on [Resource / Team]?"`  
  * *"What is the ETA on heavy rescue?"* (¿Cuál es la hora de llegada del equipo pesado?)  
  * *"What is the ETA on the search dog unit?"* (¿Cuándo llegan los perros?)  
* **Answering:** `"ETA is [time] due to [reason]."`  
  * *"ETA is 45 minutes due to road blockage."*  
  * *"ETA is 2 hours due to heavy traffic and collapsed bridges."*

### Rule 2: Reassuring Trapped Victims during Mechanical Work (Protocolo de Ruido)
Romper losas de hormigón con martillos neumáticos o sierras genera vibración y pánico extremo en una persona atrapada en un espacio confinado (*void space*).  
Bernie debe usar la regla de advertencia previa:
```
[EXPLAIN NOISE] ➔ [PROTECT AIRWAY] ➔ [CONFIRM POSITION]
"You will hear loud cutting noises." ➔ "Cover your mouth and eyes." ➔ "Keep still, we are almost there."
```

### Rule 3: Pronunciation Alerts
* **Debris** = `/dəˈbriː/` o *de-bríi* (la **S** final es muda).
* **Hydraulic** = `/haɪˈdrɒl.ɪk/` o *hai-DRÓ-lik* (empieza con sonido *hai*, no *i*).
* **Deceased** = `/dɪˈsiːst/` o *di-SÍIST* (persona fallecida, la letra **C** suena como **S** suave).

## Part 5: Operational Roleplay Scenarios / Simulaciones de Campo (15 mins)

### 📢 Scenario 1: Requesting Heavy Rescue & Negotiating ETA
* **Learner's Role:** Bernie — Ground Operations Lead at a collapsed 4-story clinic.
* **Tutor's Role:** Louie — Incident Command Dispatch on the radio.
* **Situation Card:**
  * Two survivors are located under a heavy concrete roof slab.
  * You cannot lift the slab with manual jacks.
  * The access road is partially blocked by debris; only 4x4 trucks can pass.
* **Bernie's Mission:**
  1. Radio call: *"Command, this is Bernie at Sector 2. Requesting heavy rescue!"*
  2. Explain the structural situation: *"Two victims trapped under a concrete slab. Manual tools are insufficient."*
  3. Specify equipment needed: *"We need heavy lifting airbags and hydraulic spreaders."*
  4. Ask for ETA: *"What is the ETA on the rescue squad?"*
  5. Advise on access: *"Advise the convoy: main road is blocked. Use the western access route."*
* **Tutor Opener (Dispatch):**  
  *(Radio chirp)* *"Sector 2, this is Command. We read you loud and clear. State your resource request."*

### 🔨 Scenario 2: Preparing a Trapped Survivor for Breaching & Cutting
* **Learner's Role:** Bernie — Search & Rescue Specialist speaking directly to a pinned victim.
* **Tutor's Role:** Louie — Trapped survivor (Lewis) in a confined space, terrified by vibrating concrete.
* **Situation Card:**
  * Heavy rescue has arrived with rotary saws and hydraulic spreaders.
  * The noise and vibration will be deafening.
  * You must prepare the victim mentally, check physical sensation, and protect their lungs.
* **Bernie's Mission:**
  1. Reassure the victim: *"Lewis, help is right here. The rescue team has arrived."*
  2. Warn about the noise: *"You will hear loud cutting noises and heavy machinery. Do not panic."*
  3. Physical & neurological check: *"Can you wiggle your toes? Can you feel your feet?"*
  4. Dust protection: *"Put this dust mask over your face and close your eyes."*
  5. Constant grounding: *"Stay still and breathe. We are cutting through the slab now."*
* **Tutor Opener (Trapped Victim):**  
  *(Shouting in panic over loud mechanical idling noises)*  
  *"Bernie?! What is that horrible loud noise?! Is the ceiling falling on me?!"*
